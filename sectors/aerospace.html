01 / Overview

Built for life at 35,000 feet.

Lightweight, durable components engineered for demanding aerospace environments. We produce precision formed and moulded parts for interiors, housings, covers and other applications where strength, consistency and low weight are critical.

We have worked with the largest UK carriers for over 30 years, supplying drawers, trays, bins and many more products. We regularly receive supplier awards for quality and on time service. From prototype development through to repeat production, every component is made with close attention to fit, finish and performance.

06 / Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What materials can Ertone mould for aerospace parts?

We mould polycarbonate, polypropylene, polystyrene, ABS and nylon. The right polymer depends on how the part is used, so an engineer recommends one when reviewing your drawing.

Do you hold ISO 9001 certification?

Yes. Ertone is ISO 9001 certified, and the certificate is available on the downloads page.

Can you support low volume runs and prototypes?

Yes. We can supply prototype parts before tooling, then run low or high volumes from the production tool.

What does the quote process involve?

Send a drawing, a sample or an idea. An engineer reviews it, then we send a quote with material and tooling recommendations.
