03 / Process

A single engineering thread from drawing to dispatch.

One team owns your part throughout, with no handovers between design house, toolmaker and moulder.

  1. Step 01

    Engineer review

    Drawing, sample or concept reviewed by an engineer.

  2. Step 02

    Quote submission

    DFM, material selection and prototype parts.

  3. Step 03

    Production

    Production tool designed, built and trialled.

  4. Step 04

    Finish

    Print, weld, assemble, pack and dispatch.
