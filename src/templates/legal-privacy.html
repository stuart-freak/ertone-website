What we collect

  • Details you send through our quote, contact and newsletter forms, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message.

Your drawings and files

Files attached to an enquiry are stored privately and can only be opened by our team through secure, time limited links. We treat drawings and designs as confidential and only use them to assess and quote for your project.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site and our business, under contracts that require them to protect it. These include our website host, our email delivery provider, our customer relationship management provider and, if you accept cookies, Google. Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, in which case appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details and attached files for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and customer records for as long as we need them for accounting and legal purposes. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it or send it to you in a portable format. You can also object to how we use it, and unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time. To do any of this, email {{ site.email }}.
